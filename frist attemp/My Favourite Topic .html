Introduction Dradon in D&D

Dragons are the apex predators and iconic symbols of Dungeons & Dragons (D&D), known for their immense power, magical prowess, and hoard-building habits. They are divided into three main families: Chromatic (evil), Metallic (good), and Gem (neutral), each featuring unique breath weapons, environments, and personalities.

The Nature of Dragons

Dragons in D&D are supreme, immortal apex predators driven by an absolute pride and a biological need to amass magical hoards. Their very existence is woven with raw magic, allowing them to warp landscapes around their lairs and orchestrate schemes across generations.

  1. Supreme Ego: Every dragon possesses immense pride.
  2. Living Magic: Dragons are creatures of pure magic.
  3. Regional Alterations: Powerful dragons warp reality around their lairs.
  4. Humanoid Guises: Many dragons possess the innate ability to polymorph into humanoids or beasts.
